# Demo Topics

Curated debate prompts for Two Minds. The personas are a **tech optimist** vs a **tech skeptic**, so good topics are genuinely two-sided *and* on that theme — that's what keeps the debate watchable instead of one side obviously winning.

## ⭐ Recommended (for the demo GIF)

> **Will AI create more jobs than it destroys?**

- Instantly understood by anyone scrolling.
- Genuinely contested — neither side trivially wins.
- Perfect persona fit (literally tech-optimist vs tech-skeptic).
- Meta-relevant: an AI app debating AI's impact — the self-referential angle is shareable.

## Strong alternates

| Topic | Why it works |
|---|---|
| Should social media be banned for under-16s? | Hot now, real legislation, sharp clash |
| Is remote work making us lonelier? | Relatable, emotional, both sides land |
| Will self-driving cars make roads safer? | Concrete, natural tech-vs-skeptic fit |
| Should AI be allowed to make medical decisions? | High-stakes, genuinely unresolved |
| Is cryptocurrency a net good for society? | Polarizing, plenty of ammo on both sides |

## Tips for picking your own

- Pick something **both personas can credibly argue** — avoid topics with an obvious right answer.
- Keep it **one sentence, instantly graspable** — the topic is the thumbnail hook.
- Tech-adjacent framing plays best with the current optimist/skeptic prompts.
